Simulation Results (mac_kern_tb.v)
conv1x1: we can output a pixel at every cycle. During 9 cycle, 9 pixels are outputted.
 16, 32, 48, 64, 80, 96, 112, 128, 144 
Delay between input and output valid signals is 6 cycles
5 cycle delay caused by mac.v, 1 cycle delay caused by the accumulation step at mac_kern.v
conv3x3: we can output a pixel at every 9 cycles (It takes 9 cycles to give one output.)  720
Delay between input and output valid signals is 14 cycles
5 cycle delay caused by mac.v, 9 cycle delay caused by the accumulation step at mac_kern.v
